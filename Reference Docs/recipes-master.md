# Sauces, Dressings & Salsas

## Mango Salsa

**Yield:** About 2 cups
**Best with:** Salmon, grilled seafood, chicken, tacos, rice bowls or tortilla chips

### Ingredients

- 1 ripe mango, peeled and diced
- 1/4 red onion, finely diced
- 1/2 red bell pepper, finely diced
- 1 small jalapeño, seeded and finely minced, optional
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh mint, chopped, optional
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup, optional
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### Instructions

1. Combine the mango, red onion, bell pepper, jalapeño, cilantro and mint in a bowl.
2. Add the lime juice, honey, salt and black pepper.
3. Toss gently so the mango keeps its shape.
4. Let the salsa rest for 10 to 15 minutes before serving.
5. Taste and adjust with more lime or salt as needed.

### Notes

- Make it no more than a few hours ahead for the best texture.
- For extra crunch, add diced cucumber.
- For a richer salsa, add half an avocado immediately before serving.

## Thai-Japanese Cucumber Dressing

**Yield:** Enough for 2 large cucumbers

### Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons tamari or soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 teaspoons sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon wasabi oil
- 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon freshly grated ginger
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
- 1 teaspoon lime juice
- 1/2 teaspoon chili crisp, optional
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds

### Instructions

1. Whisk the tamari, rice vinegar, sesame oil, wasabi oil, honey, ginger, garlic and lime juice together.
2. Taste before adding chili crisp. The dressing should be salty, bright, lightly sweet and aromatic rather than aggressively hot.
3. Add the sesame seeds just before tossing with the cucumbers.

### Notes

- Use gluten-free tamari when serving someone who must avoid gluten.
- Wasabi oil varies widely in strength. Start with 1/2 teaspoon if the bottle is especially pungent.
- The dressing also works on cold noodles, cabbage slaw or grilled salmon.

## Raspberry Vinaigrette

**Best with:** Chilled chicken, goat cheese and raspberry salad

### Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons raspberry jam (or 1/3 cup fresh raspberries, mashed and strained)
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar or balsamic vinegar
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon honey
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions

1. Whisk everything together until emulsified (or shake in a jar).
2. Taste — it should echo the berries in the salad without overpowering the goat cheese. Adjust honey or vinegar as needed.

### Notes

- Salad pairing: chilled chicken, goat cheese, fresh raspberries, candied pecans or walnuts, thin-sliced red onion, baby spinach or arugula.
- Lighter alternatives for the same salad: honey-balsamic, poppy seed, or lemon-honey vinaigrette.

## Bourbon-Shallot Seafood Butter

**Yield:** One ~250 g log (12 coins)
**Best with:** Chargrilled oysters, lobster — built to pair with a pour of bourbon. Naturally GF (check Worcestershire).

### Ingredients

- 250 g unsalted butter, softened
- 3 tablespoons bourbon
- 1 shallot, minced fine
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon maple syrup
- Zest of 1 lemon plus 1 teaspoon juice
- 1 teaspoon gluten-free Worcestershire
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne
- 2 tablespoons chives, minced
- 1 tablespoon flat-leaf parsley, minced
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

### Method

1. Sweat shallot and garlic in 1 tbsp of the butter over medium, 2–3 min, no browning. Add bourbon and maple; reduce to a syrupy tablespoon.
2. Cool the reduction completely — warm splits the log.
3. Beat the remaining butter until creamy; add the reduction and everything else. Taste salt against how briny your oysters run.
4. Roll into a 4 cm log in parchment; chill 2+ hours.

### Notes

- Oysters: a coin per shucked shell, screaming grill 4–5 min until the liquor bubbles. Optional parm the last minute.
- Lobster: coins under the split shell before grilling, or melt as the dipping butter.
- Raw oysters get nothing — brush melted butter on grilled bread alongside instead.
- Fridge 1 week, freezer 3 months; slice coins straight from frozen.
- Flatters wheated bourbons (Maker's, Buffalo Trace); ease the cayenne for high-rye pours.

# Marinades and Rubs

## Flank Steak Marinade — Quick (4–8 hr)

**Scale:** Coats ~5–6 lb flank (4–6 steaks, feeds ~15)

### Ingredients

- 1 cup soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 3/4 cup olive oil
- 3/4 cup brown sugar, packed
- 6 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 12 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2 teaspoons black pepper
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated (optional)
- 6 green onions, sliced (optional)

### Instructions

1. Whisk until the sugar dissolves.
2. Split steak and marinade across 2 zip bags.
3. Refrigerate 4–8 hours. Do not exceed ~12 hours at this salt/acid level.
4. Grill hot and fast; rest, then slice thin against the grain.

### Notes

- GF check: use tamari or GF soy, and confirm the Worcestershire label (Lea & Perrins Canada is GF).
- No Dijon mustard (confirmed preference).

## Flank Steak Marinade — Overnight (12–18 hr)

**Scale:** Coats ~5–6 lb flank (4–6 steaks, feeds ~15)
**v2 of the quick marinade** — less soy and vinegar so the surface doesn't over-cure, more oil to protect.

### Ingredients

- 1/2 cup soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 3/4 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
- 3 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 10 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2 teaspoons black pepper
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 6 green onions, sliced

### Instructions

1. Whisk until the sugar dissolves.
2. Divide steak and marinade between 2 large zip bags, press out air, turn to coat. Refrigerate overnight — 12–18 hours is fine at these proportions.
3. Pull steaks out 30–45 minutes before cooking. Pat dry so they sear instead of steam.
4. Grill over high direct heat, 4–5 minutes per side for medium-rare (54–57°C internal), in batches so the grill stays hot.
5. Rest 8–10 minutes, then slice thin against the grain at an angle.

# Salads

## Thai-Japanese Cucumber Salad

**Serves:** 4 as a side
**Preparation time:** 15 minutes, plus optional draining time

### Ingredients

- 2 large English cucumbers
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons tamari or soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 teaspoons sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon wasabi oil
- 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon freshly grated ginger
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
- 1 teaspoon lime juice
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
- 2 tablespoons fresh mint, roughly chopped
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon chili crisp, optional

### Instructions

1. Slice the cucumbers thinly. For more texture, lightly smash them first and cut them into irregular bite-sized pieces.
2. Toss the cucumbers with the salt and let them drain in a colander for 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Pat the cucumbers dry with a clean towel.
4. In a large bowl, whisk together the tamari, rice vinegar, sesame oil, wasabi oil, honey, ginger, garlic and lime juice.
5. Add the cucumbers, cilantro, mint and green onions.
6. Toss thoroughly and finish with sesame seeds and chili crisp.
7. Serve immediately or chill for up to 30 minutes.

### Optional Additions

- Crushed peanuts or cashews
- Thinly sliced radish
- Shredded carrot
- Edamame
- Crispy shallots
- Diced avocado

## Watermelon-Feta-Mint Salad

**Serves:** ~15 as a side (scale down freely)

### Ingredients

- 1 large watermelon (10–12 lb), cubed
- 1.5–2 lb block feta in brine, cubed or crumbled (block tastes better than pre-crumbled)
- 1 large bunch fresh mint, leaves torn
- Juice of 2–3 limes
- Olive oil, a light drizzle
- Flaky salt and black pepper

### Instructions

1. Cube the watermelon and chill.
2. Just before serving, combine watermelon, feta and mint.
3. Dress with lime juice, a light drizzle of olive oil, salt and pepper. Toss gently.

### Notes

- **Assemble day-of only** — cut watermelon weeps if it sits.
- Naturally gluten-free.

## German Creamy Cucumber-Dill Salad (Gurkensalat)

**Serves:** 6 as a side
**Best with:** Grilled meats, BBQ spreads. Naturally GF.

### Ingredients

- 2 large English cucumbers
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt (for draining)
- 1/2 cup sour cream, full fat
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt, plain full fat
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
- 3 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar or lemon juice (optional, for lift)
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar (optional, traditional)
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, plus salt to taste

### Method

1. Shave the cucumbers into thin 2–3 mm rounds.
2. Toss with the salt; drain in a colander 20–30 min, then squeeze gently in handfuls and discard the liquid.
3. Stir the dressing together; season lightly — the cucumbers bring salt from draining.
4. Fold in the cucumbers; chill 30–60 min. Serve within a few hours, extra dill on top.

### Notes

- 2:1 sour cream to yogurt keeps it light without losing richness; all sour cream for old-school.
- Dairy in summer — keep it shaded or nested in ice outdoors.

# Soups and Stews

## Gluten-Free Hungarian Beef Goulash

**Serves:** 6
A rich, paprika-forward beef stew thickened naturally with potatoes — no flour, no roux, no gluten.

### Ingredients

- 2 lb beef chuck, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil or lard
- 3 yellow onions, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tablespoons sweet Hungarian paprika (certified GF)
- 1 teaspoon smoked or hot paprika
- 1 teaspoon caraway seeds, crushed
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 red bell peppers, diced
- 3 cups beef broth (certified GF)
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 1/2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, cubed
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

### Instructions

1. **Sear the beef:** Pat the beef dry, season with salt and pepper. Heat the oil in a heavy Dutch oven over medium-high and brown the beef in batches, ~8 minutes total. Set aside.
2. **Soften the onions:** Lower heat to medium. Cook the onions until deeply softened and golden, ~10 minutes. Add garlic; cook 1 minute more.
3. **Bloom the spices:** Pull the pot **off the heat** so the paprika doesn't scorch and turn bitter. Stir in both paprikas, caraway and tomato paste for ~30 seconds until fragrant.
4. **Build the stew:** Return the beef and juices. Add peppers, broth and bay leaves. Simmer, covered, low and slow until fork-tender, ~90 minutes.
5. **Add potatoes:** Stir in the potatoes. Simmer uncovered until tender and the broth thickens from their starch, ~30 minutes.
6. **Finish:** Discard bay leaves, adjust seasoning, rest 10 minutes.

### Notes

- The three hidden-gluten checkpoints: paprika (wheat anti-caking agents in cheap blends), beef broth/bouillon, tomato paste.
- To thicken further without flour: mash a few cooked potato cubes against the pot and stir back in (the traditional Hungarian way).
- Serve with sour cream, GF egg noodles or rice. Better the next day; freezes up to 3 months.

# Sandwiches and Wraps

## Tequila Lime Chicken — Tacos or Club

**Serves:** 4
**Source:** food.com #493784. House use: taco filling; the club sandwich is the original format.

### Tequila lime marinade

- 1/4 cup tequila
- 1/2 cup lime juice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon lime zest
- 1 jalapeño
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1/4 cup cilantro, leaves and tender stems
- 1 teaspoon ancho or chipotle chile powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- Salt and black pepper (on the chicken at the grill)
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts (~4 oz each), flattened slightly with a mallet

### Method

1. Blitz everything except the chicken, salt and pepper in a food processor until smooth.
2. Bag with the chicken, massage to coat. Marinate at least 4 hours or overnight.
3. Grill medium-high on oiled grates; season with salt and pepper. ~5–7 min per side until cooked through.

### Taco build (house version)

Slice or chop the grilled chicken. Warm corn tortillas (100% corn = GF), then guacamole or avocado, mango salsa (on file — perfect match), crumbled queso fresco, cilantro, lime wedge. Chipotle mayo optional.

### Club build (original)

Toasted bun, mayo (jalapeño or chipotle: 1/2 cup mayo + 1 tbsp adobo sauce), lettuce, tomato, 2 slices crisp smoky bacon, generous guacamole, crumbled queso fresco.

### Notes

- The marinade also works over burrito bowls and salads.
- Half a cup of lime juice is a lot of acid — don't push much past overnight or the surface texture goes mealy.

# Pasta and Noodles

## Family Spaghetti Sauce (2009)

### Ingredients

- 2 lb ground beef, extra lean
- 3–4 celery sticks
- 2 peppers, red + green
- Garlic
- Tomato paste
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 can tomato juice
- 2 cans spaghetti sauce
- Herbs, including marjoram

### Method

1. Brown the beef in a large pot; drain if needed.
2. Add diced celery and peppers; soften 5–8 minutes. Garlic for the last minute.
3. Stir in tomato paste; cook 1–2 minutes to darken.
4. Add diced tomatoes, tomato juice, and both cans of sauce.
5. Season with marjoram, oregano, basil, bay, salt, pepper.
6. Simmer low and partially covered 1–2+ hours, stirring occasionally.

### Notes

- Herb quantities and method to be confirmed against the paper original.
- Big batch — freezes well.

## Seafood

### Salmon with Mango Salsa

**Serves:** 4
**Preparation time:** 15 minutes
**Cooking time:** 10 to 14 minutes

### Ingredients

#### Salmon

- 4 salmon fillets, about 170 to 200 g each
- 1 tablespoon avocado oil or olive oil
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- Zest of 1 lime

#### Mango Salsa

- 1 ripe mango, diced
- 1/4 red onion, finely diced
- 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 small jalapeño, seeded and minced, optional
- 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

### Instructions

1. Heat the oven to **220°C / 425°F**.
2. Pat the salmon dry and place it skin-side down on a lined baking sheet.
3. Rub the salmon with oil.
4. Combine the salt, smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, black pepper and lime zest. Season the salmon evenly.
5. Bake for 10 to 14 minutes, depending on thickness, until the centre flakes easily but remains moist.
6. While the salmon cooks, combine all mango salsa ingredients in a bowl.
7. Rest the salmon for 2 minutes.
8. Spoon the mango salsa generously over each fillet and serve.

### Recommended Sides

- Coconut rice
- Cilantro-lime rice
- Grilled asparagus
- Roasted sweet potatoes
- Thai-Japanese cucumber salad
- Simple cabbage slaw

### Grill Method

1. Preheat the grill to medium-high.
2. Oil the grates well.
3. Grill the salmon skin-side down for 5 to 7 minutes.
4. Flip carefully and cook for another 2 to 4 minutes.
5. Top with mango salsa after the salmon leaves the grill.

## Beef

### Flank Steak Stir-Fry with Black Bean–Hoisin Sauce

**Serves:** 3–4 (baseline 1 lb steak; tested scaled to 800 g — sear in batches at that volume)
Originally built around leftover fire-roasted vegetables (grape tomatoes, snap peas, onion, peppers) — any cooked or quick-cooking veg works.

### Ingredients

#### Velveting marinade

- 1 lb flank steak, sliced thin against the grain
- 1 tablespoon tamari
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon neutral oil
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda (optional — tenderizes)

#### Sauce

- 1 tablespoon black bean sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons hoisin
- 3 tablespoons water
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, minced

#### Everything else

- 1/3 cup cashews
- Cooked or leftover vegetables, ~2–3 cups
- Jasmine rice (microwave rice, 10 minutes, works great)
- Finishing: white sesame oil drizzled off heat; chili crisp at the table

### Instructions

1. **Marinate:** Toss the sliced steak with tamari, cornstarch, oil and baking soda. Rest 20–30 minutes at room temp.
2. **While it sits:** Mix the sauce; start the microwave rice when the steak has ~10 minutes of marinating left.
3. **Toast cashews** in a dry pan, 2–3 minutes. Remove.
4. **Sear the steak** in a hot oiled pan, 1–2 minutes. Remove. (Batches if scaled up.)
5. **Revive the veggies** in the same hot pan, 1–2 minutes — they're already cooked, just warm them.
6. **Combine:** Steak, veggies, cashews and sauce back in the pan; toss 1 minute to glaze.
7. **Plate** over rice. Finish with a drizzle of sesame oil off the heat; chili crisp on the side.

### Notes

- Wine pairing: Zinfandel first choice; Grenache lighter alternative; avoid Cabernet Sauvignon (tannin clashes with soy-based sauces).
- Premium sesame oil (e.g. Henko) is a finishing oil only — never cook with it.

# Side Dishes

## Celeriac Purée with Infused Milk & Cream

**Serves:** 4
Silky celery-root purée where the milk and cream are steeped with garlic, thyme and bay before the celeriac goes in — flavour runs all the way through. Naturally gluten-free.

### Ingredients

- 900 g celeriac (celery root), peeled, cut into 2 cm cubes
- 500 ml whole milk
- 120 ml heavy cream
- 3 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- 3 thyme sprigs
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/2 teaspoon black peppercorns
- 50 g cold unsalted butter
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Pinch of freshly grated nutmeg

### Instructions

1. **Infuse:** Combine milk, cream, garlic, thyme, bay and peppercorns in a saucepan. Warm to a bare simmer, remove from heat, steep ~15 minutes. Never let it boil hard.
2. **Simmer:** Add the celeriac to the infused liquid (aromatics still in). Gentle simmer until completely fork-tender, ~20 minutes. Keep low so the milk doesn't catch.
3. **Strain:** Lift the celeriac into a blender. Strain and reserve all the cooking liquid; discard aromatics.
4. **Blend:** Blend celeriac with the cold butter, adding reserved liquid a splash at a time until glossy and silky. Less liquid = pipeable; more = spoonable. Push through a fine sieve for true velvet.
5. **Season:** Salt, nutmeg, taste, serve warm.

### Notes

- Cooking the celeriac *in* the infused milk rather than water is the whole trick — it seasons from the inside.
- Pairs beautifully with steak; rich, so plan the rest of the plate light.

# Breakfast and Brunch

## Classic Fluffy Pancakes

**Yield:** About 10 medium pancakes
**Serves:** 3 to 4

### Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 1 large egg
- 3 tablespoons melted butter, plus more for the pan
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Instructions

1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt.
2. In a second bowl, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter and vanilla.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients.
4. Stir only until no large dry patches remain. Small lumps are desirable.
5. Let the batter rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
6. Heat a lightly buttered skillet or griddle over medium heat.
7. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake.
8. Cook until bubbles appear and the edges begin to look dry, about 2 minutes.
9. Flip and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes.
10. Serve immediately.

### Optional Additions

Add one of the following after pouring the batter onto the griddle:

- Blueberries
- Sliced banana
- Chocolate chips
- Chopped pecans
- Cinnamon
- Lemon zest

### Serving Ideas

- Maple syrup and salted butter
- Fresh berries and whipped cream
- Peanut butter and banana
- Warm apple compote
- Mango salsa for a sweet-savoury variation

# Drinks and Cocktails

## Vișinată (Romanian Sour Cherry Liqueur)

**Status:** In progress — final quantities pending batch size decision.

### Standard framework (per 4 L jar batch)

- ~2 kg sour cherries (vișine), stems removed, unpitted
- ~700 g–1 kg sugar (to taste)
- ~1.5–2 L spirit

### Method outline

1. Layer cherries and sugar in a large jar; let macerate in a sunny spot until the sugar draws out the juice (a few days to 2 weeks).
2. Add the spirit, seal, and rest in a cool dark place 6–8 weeks minimum, shaking occasionally.
3. Strain, bottle, and age further — it improves markedly at 3+ months.

### Notes

- Starting proof matters: cherries and sugar dilute the final ABV significantly. A 76% spirit (e.g. Spirytus Gdański, if LCBO stock confirms) lands near the traditional 28–32% finished strength; standard 40% vodka gives a lighter, ~18–22% result — still good, just softer.
- Two 750 ml bottles of high-proof spirit ≈ one 4 L batch.

# Ingredient Notes

## Wasabi Oil and Gluten

Wasabi oil is often gluten-free because it is generally made from oil and wasabi flavouring. However, formulations vary.

Check the label for:

- Wheat
- Barley
- Malt
- Regular soy sauce
- A "may contain wheat" warning
- A certified gluten-free statement when required for celiac disease

When using soy sauce in these recipes, choose **gluten-free tamari** when gluten avoidance is necessary.

## Hidden Gluten at the Grill (from Father's Day planning)

- **Shared grill grates:** scrub hot or use foil if breaded/marinated gluten food was cooked on them.
- **Bottled sauces:** coleslaw dressing, Worcestershire (barley malt in many), regular soy sauce, many BBQ sauces. Anything mixed yourself from oil/citrus/herbs is safe by default.
- **Serving utensils:** keep separate so gluten-eating guests don't double-dip GF dishes.

## Goulash Gluten Checkpoints

Paprika (cheap blends may use wheat anti-caking filler), beef broth/bouillon, tomato paste.
