Three ways to read “professional French kitchen”

Each column is the same recipe screen — Celeriac Purée, real content from the app — rendered through a different palette. The markup is identical in all three; only the nine design tokens change. That is exactly how the real switch works.

Pick one and I'll build it. Light theme only, as agreed. Type is approximated here with system faces — the app's own Fraunces / Work Sans / Spline Sans Mono can't load inside this preview, so judge the colour, not the letterforms.

Celeriac Purée with Infused Milk & CreamGF

Serves 4 · the milk and cream steep with garlic, thyme and bay

Scale − 6 + servings base 4

Ingredients

  • 1.35 kgceleriac, peeled, 2 cm cubes
  • 750 mlwhole milk
  • 180 mlheavy cream
  • 75 gcold unsalted butter
  • —pinch of freshly grated nutmeg

Method

  1. 1Infuse: warm the milk and cream with garlic, thyme and bay to a bare simmer. Never a hard boil.
  2. 2Simmer: add the celeriac, aromatics still in, until completely fork-tender.

Notes

  • — Cooking it in the infused milk rather than water is the whole trick.

What actually changes in the code

Whichever you pick, the work is the same shape. Web has nine variables in one 66-line file, web/src/lib/tokens.css, driving 165 usages across six screens. iOS mirrors them in DesignSystem/Theme.swift, which is impressively disciplined — those nine hex values are the only colour literals in all 37 Swift files.

Two bits of cleanup ride along. Sixteen hardcoded colours in the Svelte routes currently dodge the tokens — #F4F3EC appears nine times, there are three translucent white chips on the scale bar, and the editor has eight literal bg-white inputs. Those become tokens, which is what makes a future dark mode a small job rather than a rewrite. And --green / --green-deep get renamed to --primary / --primary-deep, so the names stop lying about what colour they hold.

Beyond the two token files: the PWA theme_color in the manifest and app.html, the four-colour logo.svg, the raster app icons, the iOS AccentColor.colorset, the QR fill in api/app/routers/recipes.py, and the palette spec in CLAUDE.md §7.
